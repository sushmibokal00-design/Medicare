Universal Sick-Day Dietary Golden Rules

1
Hydrate with Electrolytes

Water alone isn't enough when sweating or experiencing digestive upset; include trace sodium, potassium, and magnesium.

2
Bland Carbohydrates First

When the digestive tract is strained, prioritize white rice, oats, crackers, and bananas to prevent gastric workload.

3
Zero Ultra-Processed Fats

Deep-fried vegetable oils spark arachidonic inflammatory cascades that prolong recovery and delay stomach emptying.

4
Cut Concentrated Sugar

Excess sucrose and high-fructose syrups suppress phagocytic white blood cell activity for up to 4 hours after ingestion.

Essential Health Habits

The Public Wellbeing Do's & Don'ts

Simple, high-impact behaviors that dramatically shorten recovery times and safeguard your family and community from cross-infection.

✓ WHAT TO DO (Proven Best Practices)

✓
Prioritize Unbroken Stage-3 & REM Sleep

During deep sleep, your bone marrow and spleen release defensive cytokines. Strive for 8 to 10 hours in a dark, quiet room.

✓
Monitor Vitals & Maintain a Temperature Log

Check oral or tympanic temperature twice daily. Document exact hours and values in case a clinical consultation becomes necessary.

✓
Use Saline Mists & Warm Saltwater Gargles

Physical hypertonic rinses draw water out of edematous inflamed mucous membranes and flush out viral debris naturally.

✓
Read OTC Drug Labels for Single vs. Multi-Action

Check active ingredients carefully. If taking a cough syrup with acetaminophen, do not take separate paracetamol pills.

✕ WHAT NOT TO DO (Common Pitfalls)

✕
DO NOT Demand Antibiotics for Viral Infections

Antibiotics attack bacterial cell walls; they have zero effect on common cold, flu, or viral sore throats and damage gut microflora.

✕
DO NOT Perform Strenuous High-Intensity Workouts

The "sweat it out" gym myth is dangerous. Intense physical exertion elevates cortisol and diverts metabolic resources from immune repair.

✕
DO NOT Take NSAIDs on an Empty Stomach

Ibuprofen, aspirin, and naproxen inhibit protective gastric prostaglandins. Always ingest with meals to avoid gastric ulcers.

✕
DO NOT Mask High-Risk Symptoms with Excess Painkillers

If fever or pain spikes despite scheduled analgesics, do not double the dose. Contact a physician immediately.

Urgent Safety Alert

When It Is NOT a Minor Illness: Emergency Triage

If you or a loved one experience any of the warning signs on the right, DO NOT attempt home care. Contact local emergency medical services or head immediately to the nearest Emergency Department.

🚨 USA / Canada: 911 🚨 Europe / India: 112 🚨 UK: 999
⚠️ Crushing, heavy chest pain or pressure radiating to arm, back, or jaw.
⚠️ Severe shortness of breath, struggling to finish a sentence, or blue lips.
⚠️ Sudden onset of explosive headache ("thunderclap") or confusion / slurred speech.
⚠️ High fever over 103°F (39.4°C) with neck stiffness and sensitivity to light.
⚠️ Vomiting blood, passing black tarry stools, or inability to keep fluids down for 24+ hours.
